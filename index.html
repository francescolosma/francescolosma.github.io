Francesco Losma

I am a DPhil (PhD) student in Economics at the University of Oxford and a member of University College, supervised by Prof. Barbara Petrongolo.

My research interests lie in labour and development economics, with a focus on matching, search frictions, and amenities. I am also interested in the economics of education, migration, and mental health.

Previously, I obtained an MPhil in Economics from the University of Oxford, an MSc in Economics from Bocconi University and a BSc in Finance from the Catholic University of Milan.
